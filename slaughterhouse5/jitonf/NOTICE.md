# NOTICE — jitonf (Slaughterhouse5 / I-13 program)

This repository (`slaughterhouse5-jitonf`) is the **`jitonf`** module of the
**I-13 program**, which is part of the **Slaughterhouse5** operating-system
project.

## Authorship

- **Human director:** David Lee Wise
- **AI co-author:** Hermes Agent (Nous Research)

The `jitonf` engine is a faithful Python port of the I-13 reference machine
published at `rgiskard01-fiddler/Fiddler` (`I,Robot/machine/i13-two-scopes.html`).
The port was produced with the assistance of Hermes Agent.

## Provenance, not derivation

Crediting the AI as co-author is a statement of **provenance** — who helped
build this artifact — and is **not** a claim that the I-13 design was derived
from, or ingested out of, any external training corpus. The I-13 language and
the IVM-13-S machine are the work of the human director; the AI's role was
implementation and transcription of the reference.
